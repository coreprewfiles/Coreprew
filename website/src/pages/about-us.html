Who we are

Storytellers rooted in two cultures

Coreprew Films was started by a group of photography enthusiasts inspired by Sydney and Bhaktapur, and by their architecture and celebrations.

Our roots in Bhaktapur, a city full of history and artistic expression, shape how we capture each person, event and project. We've always mixed traditional artistry with modern style. Today we cover a wide range of stories: lively street life, love stories at weddings, and everything in between.

How we work

Quality, trust & respect

We love capturing real moments, emotions and experiences, without staging them to fit a narrative.

Capturing the essence

Every moment has a spirit, however brief. We aim to capture it, creating images that are both artistically and emotionally stunning.

Cultural respect

Shaped by the traditions of Australia and Nepal, we approach every ceremony with care and respect so your photos feel authentic.

Innovation & artistry

Rooted in tradition, we keep up with the latest techniques and equipment to give you the best photography and cinematography.
