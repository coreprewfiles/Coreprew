Questions

Good to know before you book

I'm ready to book. What happens next?

Send us your date using the booking form, message us on WhatsApp or call 0493 843 768. We'll set up a quick meeting to go over the details and pricing, then lock in your date.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Yes. A 50% non-refundable deposit secures your date and time. If you need to reschedule after paying the deposit, there's a fee of 10% of the total, and the new date depends on our availability. The remaining balance is paid before your photos and videos are handed over.

How long until I receive my photos and videos?

Every photo and video is individually retouched and edited, so timing depends on the amount of content and the style you choose. We aim to deliver within 1–2 months after your event.

Do you make photo albums and prints?

Yes, we make albums and prints. Let us know what you'd like when you book and we'll include it in your quote.

Where are you based, and do you travel?

We have teams in Sydney (Mount Druitt, NSW) and in Bhaktapur, Nepal, and we're happy to travel for your event. In Nepal, travel is included within the Kathmandu Valley; events more than 20 km from Kathmandu city have an extra travel cost. For travel within Australia, just ask and we'll include it in your quote.

Can I create a custom package?

Absolutely. Our listed packages are a starting point. Tell us your ideas and we'll create a personalised quote that works for you.
